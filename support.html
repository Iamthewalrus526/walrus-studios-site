Support

Need help with one of our apps, found a bug, or have an idea? We'd love to hear from you.

Email support@walrusappstudios.com

To help us fix things quickly, please include:

  • The app name and version (usually in the app's Settings or About screen)
  • Your device model and iOS, iPadOS, or macOS version
  • What happened, and what you expected to happen
  • A screenshot, if it helps

Refunds

App Store purchases are handled by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

We usually reply within two business days.
