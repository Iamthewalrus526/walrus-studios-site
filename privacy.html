Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

Walrus Studios LLC ("Walrus Studios," "we," "us") makes apps for Apple devices. This policy explains what information our apps and this website collect, and how we use it. It applies to all of our apps unless an app's App Store listing or in-app notice says otherwise.

Information we collect

Our apps are designed to work without collecting personal information. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal information.

  • Data you create in our apps is stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud, it may sync through your own iCloud account, which we cannot access.
  • Anonymous diagnostics. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. These do not identify you.
  • Messages you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to respond to you.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment information.

Third-party services

Our apps do not include third-party advertising or tracking. If a future app uses a third-party service, we will update this policy and the app's App Store privacy details before that change takes effect.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

This website

This site does not use cookies or analytics. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and reliability.

Your choices

You can delete an app's data at any time by deleting the app from your device. To ask about or delete any messages you've sent us, contact us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
